Meet your esthetician

Hi, I’m Elizabeth.

Skincare has always been personal for me. I grew up with sensitive skin and eczema, and long before esthetics school I was researching ingredients and helping friends and family build their routines. In my twenties I left retail to follow that passion, went back to school for esthetics, and fell in love with it right away.

I’ve been licensed for two years. I started at a med spa specializing in radio frequency microneedling, where I learned how skin heals and rebuilds, how to treat texture, scarring and uneven tone safely, and why prep and aftercare matter as much as the treatment itself. I opened Santo to create a space where people feel safe, relaxed and genuinely good about themselves, not just their skin.

My specialty is Korean skincare. Every product I use is Korean, chosen for its holistic philosophy and advanced innovation. My treatment room is calm and peaceful, but the conversation flows naturally too. Leave the outside world behind, settle into total zen mode, and walk out glowing.
